THE GAP

Buyers can't picture it.
So they don't commit.

Off-plan sales live and die on imagination. A PDF floor plan asks a buyer to do the hardest thing in property: emotionally commit to a space that doesn't exist yet.

So you commission renders. And then you wait.

Weeks, not days
